Conduct a rigorous interview to sharpen the plan or design. Ask one focused question at a time, challenge assumptions, and make trade-offs explicit. As decisions are made, draft or update architecture decision records (ADRs) and maintain a glossary of agreed terms.

Keep a running decision log that captures every decision the user makes, including small ones. For each entry, record the question, the agent’s recommendation and rationale, and the user’s final decision and any rationale they provide. Mark open questions as pending and link to the relevant ADR where applicable.
