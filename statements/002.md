16 Leadership Principles
Amazon’s interview process is deeply rooted in its leadership principles. These aren’t just wall decorations, they’re the actual criteria by which you’ll be evaluated. Here’s a quick overview of each principle:

Customer Obsession: Start with the customer and work backward, earning and keeping their trust.
Ownership: Think long-term as an owner, not sacrificing future value for short-term results.
Invent and Simplify: Innovate, simplify, and be open to ideas from anywhere.
Are Right, A Lot: Show strong judgment, have good instincts, and seek diverse perspectives.
Learn and Be Curious: Never stop learning and exploring new possibilities.
Hire and Develop the Best: Raise the bar with every hire and develop leaders.
Insist on the Highest Standards: Set standards others might find unreasonably high.
Think Big: Create bold directions and look around corners for ways to serve customers.
Bias for Action: Move quickly, take calculated risks, and don’t over-analyze reversible decisions.
Frugality: Accomplish more with less through resourcefulness and self-sufficiency.
Earn Trust: Listen attentively, speak candidly, treat others respectfully, and be self-critical.
Dive Deep: Stay connected to details, audit frequently, and be skeptical when metrics and anecdotes differ.
Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit: Challenge decisions respectfully, have conviction, but commit once decided.
Deliver Results: Focus on key inputs and deliver with quality and timeliness despite setbacks.
Strive to be Earth’s Best Employer: Create a safe, productive, diverse work environment with empathy.
Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility: Be humble about the impact and leave things better than found.
